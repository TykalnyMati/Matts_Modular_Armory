# Weapon Augments

## Weapon augments can be inserted only into weapons

# Sharpness Augment:
Increases overall melee damage

Incompatible with other damaging effects

<Recipe id="sharpness_augment" />

# Smite Augment:
Increases damage dealt to undead mobs

Incompatible with other damaging effects

<Recipe id="smite_augment" />

# Bane of Arthropods Augment:
Increases damage dealt and applies Slowness to arthropods

Incompatible with other damaging effects

<Recipe id="bane_of_arthropods_augment" />

# Impaling Augment:
Increases damage dealt to aquatic mobs

Incompatible with other damaging effects

<Recipe id="impaling_augment" />

# Looting Augment:
Increases the amount and rarity of mob drops

<Recipe id="looting_augment" />

# Knockback Augment:
Increases the weapon's knockback

<Recipe id="knockback_augment" />

# Fire Aspect Augment:
Sets hit entities ablaze upon attack

Duration increases with level

Each level adds 4 seconds of burning

<Recipe id="fire_aspect_augment" />

# Sweeping Edge Augment:
Increases the damage of sweeping attacks

Can only be applied to swords

<Recipe id="sweeping_edge_augment" />

# Exile Augment:
Increases damage dealt to raiders (and vexes)

Incompatible with other damaging effects

<Recipe id="exile_augment" />

# Poison Augment:
Applies Poison to hit entities

- Level I: Poison I | 6 seconds
- Level II: Poison II | 4 seconds

<Recipe id="poison_augment" />

# Wither Augment:
Applies Wither to hit entities

- Level I: Wither I | 9 seconds
- Level II: Wither II | 6 seconds
- Level III: Wither III | 4 seconds

<Recipe id="wither_augment" />

# Life Sense Augment:
Right-clicking with the weapon applies Glowing to surrounding entities

- Level I: 8 block radius | 3 second duration
- Level II: 16 block radius | 6 second duration
- Level III: 24 block radius | 9 second duration
- Level IV: 32 block radius | 12 second duration

Uses 5 durability with a 1-second cooldown.

<Recipe id="life_sense_augment" />

# Overdrive Augment:
Grants Strength when used

- Level I: Strength I | 40 seconds
- Level II: Strength II | 30 seconds
- Level III: Strength III | 20 seconds

Uses Level * 10 durability with 60-second cooldown

<Recipe id="overdrive_augment" />
