# Armor augments

## Armor augments can be inserted into all armor pieces
## But there are augments that can be inserted only into certain armor pieces

### All protections work like their enchantment counterparts, higher levels allow for mixing and matching different augments on different armor pieces without losing out on regular protection, check out the [vanilla minecraft wiki](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Protection#Usage) to see how damage reduction is calculated

# Protection Augment:
Reduces overall damage taken

Incompatible with Blast Protection, Projectile Protection and Fire Protection

<Recipe id="protection_augment" />

# Blast Protection Augment:
Reduces damage taken from explosions

Reduces knockback taken from explosions and wind charges

Incompatible with Protection, Projectile Protection and Fire Protection

<Recipe id="blast_protection_augment" />

# Projectile Protection Augment:
Reduces damage taken from projectiles

Incompatible with Protection, Blast Protection and Fire Protection

<Recipe id="projectile_protection_augment" />

# Fire Protection Augment:
Reduces damage taken from fire

Reduces the amount of time the wearer stays on fire

*As a bonus the augment item is immune to fire*

Incompatible with Protection, Blast Protection and Projectile Protection

<Recipe id="fire_protection_augment" />

# Thorns Augment:
Chance to damage attackers at the cost of extra durability

Each armor piece has Level * 15% chance of dealing damage

Consumes 2 durability when activated

<Recipe id="thorns_augment" />

# Vitality Augment:
Increases max health

Level 1 increases health by 1 heart

Level 2 increases health by 2.5 hearts

*bonus health unfortunately gets reset on reload*

<Recipe id="vitality_augment" />

# Gilded Augment:
Piglin become neutral towards you just like if you were wearing a piece of golden armor

Remember not to anger them in other ways

Incompatible with Super Gilded
