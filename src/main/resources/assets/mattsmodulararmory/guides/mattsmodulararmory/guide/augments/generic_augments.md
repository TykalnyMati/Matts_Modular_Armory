# Generic Augments

## Generic augments can be inserted into all modular items

# Unbreaking Augment:
Reduces the chance of consuming durability by 12.5% for each level, this also applies to special actions (like right-clicking) 

Items with 8 augments become unbreakable

<Recipe id="unbreaking_augment" />

# Mending Augment:
Repairs items for 2 durability for every xp point absorbed when the item is held in hand or equipped

Incompatible with XP Siphon

<Recipe id="mending_augment" />

# Magnet Augment:
Attracts items in the range of 4 block to the player when the item is held or equipped

<Recipe id="magnet_augment" />

# XP Siphon Augment:
Repairs items for 2 durability for every xp point absorbed from the player's xp bar when the item is held in hand or equipped

Incompatible with Mending

<Recipe id="xp_siphon_augment" />
